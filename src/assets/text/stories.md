**The Real and Fake Heiress**



Ivy was in her third month as an intern at Starlight Corporation when the CEO called her into the office. The CEO, Victoria, was Ivy's direct supervisor and the undisputed heiress of the company. Victoria slammed a document into Ivy's face and barked, "You dare steal my design to enter a competition? You're just a poor girl from the countryside, what do you know about high-end jewelry?" Before Ivy could explain, Victoria grabbed a cup of coffee and threw it all over Ivy. "Get out! You're fired."



The next day, Starlight Corporation held its annual gala. Victoria wore a custom gown and gave a speech under the spotlight, basking in everyone's flattery. Ivy, wearing a washed-out shirt, came to collect her final paycheck but was stopped by security at the door. Victoria walked off the stage, deliberately strutted up to Ivy, pulled a stack of cash from her purse, and smashed it into Ivy's face. "This is enough to send you back to the countryside to farm. Don't dirty my eyes again." The socialites and colleagues around covered their mouths and laughed.



Just then, the doors of the hall were thrown open. A middle-aged man in a black trench coat, followed by twelve bodyguards in sunglasses, walked straight toward Ivy. The man was Richard, the head of a multinational megacorporation. He walked up to Ivy, suddenly dropped to one knee, and presented a top-grade imperial green jade necklace with both hands. "Young Miss, I have been looking for you for twenty years. I finally found you."



Dead silence fell over the room. Victoria froze, then stammered, "Mr... Mr. Richard, are you mistaken? She's just an intern under me!" Richard stood up and delivered a resounding slap across Victoria's face, sending her stumbling to the ground. "Who the hell do you think you are? Twenty years ago, your mother—who was just a maid—swapped my daughter and sent her to the slums, letting a fake like you take my daughter's place for twenty years!" Richard turned to the chairman of Starlight Corporation: "I just bought this company. Now, get this fake heiress out of my sight immediately." Victoria collapsed to the floor, pale as death. Ivy looked at the cash on the ground, stepped on it, and walked out the door without looking back.



**The War God Security Guard**



At the St. John City Psychiatric Hospital, security guard Ethan was sweeping the floor. A black Rolls-Royce pulled up to the entrance, and several thugs armed with steel pipes dragged a girl in a wedding dress inside. The girl was Chloe, the daughter of the city's richest man. The thug leader kicked over the reception desk and roared, "Anyone who calls the cops, I'll kill you!" The director hid under the desk, trembling.



The thugs pushed Chloe into a corner and were about to take photos to extort her. Ethan walked over with his broom and said calmly, "This is a hospital. Keep your voices down." The thug leader laughed, pointed at Ethan's nose, and cursed, "You're just a damn security guard, who the hell are you pretending to be? Believe it or not, I'll stab you right now!" With that, the thug pulled out a dagger and thrust it toward Ethan's stomach.



Ethan didn't dodge. He reached out with two fingers and caught the blade dead in the air. With a clank, the dagger snapped in two. Then, Ethan delivered a front kick that sent the 200-pound thug leader flying ten meters across the room, slamming into the wall and knocking him unconscious. The remaining thugs tried to swarm him. Ethan snatched a steel pipe and, in a matter of seconds, broke all their legs. The room was filled with wails of agony.



Chloe covered her mouth in shock. Ethan didn't look at her; instead, he pulled an old communicator from his pocket and dialed a number. "This is Ethan. Prepare to clean up the scene." Three minutes later, a massive roar came from outside the hospital. Five black armed helicopters hovered over the building. Dozens of fully armed mercenaries rappelled down, lined up at the hospital entrance, and saluted in unison: "Commander, we apologize for the delay!" The director crawled out from under the desk, so scared he wet his pants, and knelt on the ground kowtowing: "Comm... Commander, I didn't know it was you..." Ethan took off his security uniform, draped it over Chloe's shoulders, and said, "This hospital is closed as of today."



**Rebirth and Revenge**



Lillian was awakened by a cold draft. She snapped her eyes open and found herself sitting in a Western restaurant, with her boyfriend of seven years, David, sitting across from her. David pushed a bank card toward her and said impatiently, "Lillian, let's break up. Helen (the heiress) has agreed to marry me. There's five thousand dollars on this card, consider it compensation for putting you through grad school."



Just a second ago, Lillian was dying of kidney failure in a freezing rental apartment. Her kidney had been forcibly harvested by David at a black-market clinic and given to Helen, the sick daughter of a wealthy tycoon. Lillian looked down at her intact body. She had been reborn, back to the day David asked for a breakup.



Lillian didn't cry or make a scene. She took the bank card and said coldly, "Fine." David was taken aback, then sneered, "You know what's good for you." He stood up to leave. Lillian pulled out her phone, directly dialed the police, and put it on speaker: "Hello, I want to report an MIT medical PhD student, David, for academic fraud, buying and selling papers, and embezzling his advisor's research funds."



David's face instantly turned pale. He lunged to snatch the phone. "Are you crazy! What nonsense are you spouting!" Lillian stood up, dodged his hand, and dialed Helen's father's number: "Chairman, is it hard to find a match for your daughter? Actually, David has known for a long time that I am a perfect match for her. He deliberately hid this from you because he wanted to hold you hostage and demand an extra estate. Do you think he truly loves your daughter?" A furious roar erupted from the other end of the line. David dropped to his knees with a thud, clinging to Lillian's leg and weeping. Lillian kicked him away, stepped out of the restaurant in her high heels, and never looked back.



**The Hidden Boss**



Sterling Group's boardroom. Cecilia was pinned to the floor by security guards. Her sister, Beatrice, dressed in a tailored suit, looked down at her and said, "Sister, it must have been hard for you to marry that vegetable CEO in my place. Now that the CEO is dead, the company is mine. You're leaving with nothing." All the shareholders chimed in, mocking Cecilia as a jinx who killed her husband.



Cecilia struggled to her feet and wiped the blood from the corner of her mouth. Years ago, when their family business was on the brink of bankruptcy, she was forced to marry the dying CEO of Sterling Group in her sister's place. For the past two years, she had been working day and night to revive the company. Now that the CEO was dead, her sister wanted to snatch the fruits of her labor.



Cecilia clapped her hands. The boardroom doors opened, and four men in suits, along with several lawyers, walked in. The leading man walked up to Cecilia and bowed respectfully: "Madam, the representatives from the Rothschild family and Wall Street consortium are in position." Cecilia walked to the head of the table, sat down, and pulled a document from her bag. "Beatrice, you probably don't know that Sterling Group went bankrupt a year ago. For the past two years, it was my overseas fund filling your bottomless pit."



Beatrice screamed, "Impossible! Where did you get the money!" Cecilia ignored her and pressed a remote. On the massive screen in the boardroom, a picture appeared—the supposedly "dead" vegetable CEO was sitting in a wheelchair, smiling at the camera: "Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to reintroduce you. Cecilia is my wife, and the only person in the world who controls the global underground financial network. Anyone who upsets my wife, I will ruin you." Beatrice's legs gave way, and she collapsed onto the floor.



**The Flash Marriage to a Billionaire**



Outside the city registry office, it was raining heavily. Grace, in her wedding dress, was publicly humiliated by her fiancé and her best friend: "You're not even worth a toe of Isabella's. Get lost!" The two of them hugged and got into a Porsche, driving away.



Grace stood in the rain, soaked to the bone. Just then, a Rolls-Royce Phantom worth thirty million dollars pulled up in front of her. The door opened, and the driver stepped out with a black umbrella. Grace threw caution to the wind, opened the door, and got in. "Sir, to the registry office. Want to get a marriage certificate?" The man in the back seat put down his newspaper. He wore a sharp suit, had deep-set features, and was Alexander, the richest man on the Forbes list.



Alexander glanced at her: "Alright."



They walked straight into the city registry, took a red-background photo, and got their marriage certificate. Two days later, Alexander took Grace to a top-tier business gala. Her cheating ex-fiancé and best friend were holding champagne and mingling. Seeing Grace in an ordinary dress, they immediately walked over to mock her: "What, you came here to hook a rich guy? A woman like you can't even afford a glass of wine here."



As soon as the words left his mouth, the gala host rushed onto the stage and excitedly announced: "Please welcome our gala's biggest investor, Mr. Alexander, to give a speech!" Amidst everyone's shocked gazes, Alexander walked onto the stage, then turned and walked straight toward Grace. In front of everyone, he took Grace's hand, kissed the back of it, and said, "Ladies and gentlemen, this is my wife."



The ex-fiancé's glass shattered on the floor, his face ashen. Alexander gave him a cold glance: "I believe I just heard someone looking down on my wife?" The next day, the ex-fiancé's family business went bankrupt. As for Grace, she was lying on a beach chair on Alexander's private island, sipping a hundred-thousand-dollar glass of juice.



**Stockpiling for the Apocalypse**



There were three days left before the zombie outbreak. Just a second ago, Zoe had her neck bitten off by a zombie. The next second, she was reborn in her own apartment. Without any hesitation, she immediately listed her apartment online at a rock-bottom price to sell it quickly, then maxed out ten credit cards with all her savings.



Zoe rented a massive abandoned warehouse on the outskirts of town and contacted all suppliers overnight. Fifty tons of rice, fifty tons of flour, tons of frozen meat, plus enough purified water, generators, solar panels, and cold medicine to last ten years. Because she had bound a "spatial system" she accidentally acquired in her past life, she stored all of it in a static pocket dimension.



Three days later, the apocalypse arrived. Water and electricity were cut off, and the city fell into the roars of zombies and the screams of humans. Zoe turned the warehouse into an impenetrable safe house. Half a month later, a group of survivors smashed open Zoe's door. Leading them was her vicious coworker from her past life and the ex-boyfriend who had abandoned her.



They were sallow and gaunt. They watched Zoe sitting in her safe house with the AC on, a bubbling pot of top-grade Wagyu beef hotpot on the table. The ex-boyfriend swallowed his drool, his eyes red as he charged forward to snatch it: "Zoe! Hand over the food or I'll kill you!" Zoe sat on the sofa, took a bite of tender beef, and snapped her fingers. The safe house instantly activated its highest defense level, and a high-voltage electric grid instantly electrocuted the ex-boyfriend into a charred crisp. The remaining survivors were so terrified they knelt on the ground begging for mercy. Zoe looked at them coldly: "You want to eat? There are plenty of zombies outside. Go fight them for it." With that, she pressed the door close button and went back to binge-watching her shows in absolute comfort.
